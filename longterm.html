Writen policy

For long-term projects , revenue share is accepted only if you provide research and proof that your game will succeed. You must also prove your experience by showing previously published games that performed well. If market research is lacking, an upfront fee will be applied. Once terms are set, payment amounts cannot be lowered, and your payment method must be clearly stated.
